# Image credits

Every photo or logo added to `assets/images/` from the web gets a row here.
No AI-generated images.

Brand logos (PayPal, Visa, Mastercard, Google, Facebook) are used only to show
that payment or sign-in option. The trademarks belong to their owners.
Visa and Mastercard are single-colour renders of the Simple Icons SVGs
(240x240 PNG, black on transparent); the app tints them with the brand colour
in light mode and white in dark mode. PayPal is the full-colour wordmark, shown
in its own colours in light mode and white in dark mode.
